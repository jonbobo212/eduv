Transfer pathways

Start in Malaysia. Graduate anywhere.

The cheapest way into a British, American or Swiss degree is to begin in Kuala Lumpur. You pay Malaysian fees for the early years and transfer with full credit.

2 + 2

Malaysia → United Kingdom

Two years at APU, two at De Montfort University. You finish with a dual award recognised across Europe.

1 + 3

Malaysia → United States

One year at INTI, then three at any of 100+ American partner universities. A full US degree at a fraction of the cost.

3 + 1

Malaysia → Switzerland

Three years at City University, final year in Switzerland — built for business and hospitality students.
